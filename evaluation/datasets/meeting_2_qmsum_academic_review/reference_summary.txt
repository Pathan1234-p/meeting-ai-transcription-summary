Dr. Elena and Marcus reviewed attention latency benchmarks for large language models on the GPU cluster. Because standard multi-head attention failed with out-of-memory errors beyond 8,000 tokens, they benchmarked FlashAttention-2 which demonstrated a 3.5x speedup. The team decided to standardize on FlashAttention-2 across all models. Marcus was assigned to run the training benchmark across eight H100 GPUs by Thursday, and gradient checkpointing trade-offs must be documented in the paper draft.
